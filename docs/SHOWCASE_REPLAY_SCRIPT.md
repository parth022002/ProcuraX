## Executive Summary & Guiding Principles

ProcuraX is an enterprise-grade **governed decision intelligence platform** designed for resilient supply chain orchestration. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets and black-box recommendations with a verifiable, multi-stage decision pipeline.

### Core Architectural Boundaries
1. **Separation of Concerns:** ML predicts disruption probability ($P(\text{disruption})$); a deterministic impact engine calculates rupee financial exposure; a constraint solver optimizes recovery actions; RAG copilot retrieves clause citations; an 11-step state machine orchestrates workflow; a Policy Engine enforces RBAC spend thresholds; human executives retain sole approval authority.
2. **Read-Only ERP Safety Guarantee:** Approved mitigation plans generate cryptographically signed, proposal-only action packets (`is_proposal_only: True`). There is **strictly zero automated ERP or purchase order mutation**.
3. **Data Provenance Transparency:** Every datum is explicitly classified as `PUBLIC_REAL`, `SYNTHETIC`, `DERIVED`, or `ILLUSTRATIVE`.
