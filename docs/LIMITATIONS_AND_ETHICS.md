## 1. Ethical Boundaries & Synthetic Reality Separation

### Synthetic Enterprise Entities
- **ApexDrive Components Pvt. Ltd. (`SUP-1047`)**, **NovaHome Appliances**, **ElectroMotion Systems India Pvt. Ltd. (`SUP-1012`)**, and all associated assembly plants (Pune, Chennai, Greater Noida) are **entirely fictional, synthetic entities** created specifically for research, validation, and PoC demonstration purposes.
- Any resemblance to real commercial entities, past or present corporate entities, or living individuals is purely coincidental.
- Internal operational records—including Purchase Orders, Bills of Materials (BOM), plant inventory runways, defect logs, and contract texts—are algorithmically generated by reproducible random walks and domain templates (`seed=42`).

### Mapping Real Public Signals to Fictional Entities
- External signal connectors ingest real public feeds from approved providers: **GDELT Project**, **US SEC EDGAR**, **Marketaux**, **NewsData.io**, and **Alpha Vantage**.
- **Crucial Rule:** The platform **never asserts that a real-world external event directly damages, impacts, or legally implicates any real entity**.
- All correlations between real macroeconomic or commodity signals (e.g., copper mining strikes, semiconductor lead time spikes) and `SUP-1047` are explicitly declared as **PoC Scenario Assumptions**:
  > *"Scenario assumption: Real public external signal mapped to fictional supplier for PoC decision replay only. Not an assertion of real-world liability."*
- Every external signal displayed in the UI or processed by the agent explicitly retains original publisher citations, canonical URLs, and distinct `published_at` vs. `retrieved_at` timestamps.

## 2. Four-Tier Data Provenance Taxonomy

To prevent data ambiguity and maintain verifiable truthfulness, every data asset and model feature within ProcuraX is tagged with one of four provenance classifications:

| Classification | Meaning & Scope | Examples in ProcuraX |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `PUBLIC_REAL` | Verified public external signal from authoritative open APIs or regulatory filings. | GDELT event articles, SEC 10-Q filing feeds, commodity price indices. |
| `SYNTHETIC` | High-fidelity internal enterprise operational data generated via deterministic scripts. | Supplier registry (`supplier.csv`), PO records (`purchase_order.csv`), plant stocks (`inventory.csv`). |
| `DERIVED` | Quantitative outputs computed by deterministic mathematics, solvers, or models. | Governed risk score (0–100), inventory runway days, net avoided loss, proposal packet hash. |
| `ILLUSTRATIVE` | High-level presentation aids, mock UI flows, and contextual demo scripts. | Showcase replay prompts, mock CPO avatar badges, UI status chips. |

## 3. Core Operational Safeguards & Safety Guarantees

### 1. Read-Only ERP Safety Guarantee
- ProcuraX **does not perform live writes or automated mutations** to enterprise Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) databases, Material Requirements Planning (MRP) systems, or financial ledgers.
- The state machine culminates in a **Proposal-Only Action Packet** (`is_proposal_only: True`). This packet is cryptographically signed (SHA-256) and routed to a staging queue for explicit manual review by enterprise procurement staff.

### 2. Separation of Mathematical and Statistical Concerns
- **ML Disruption Probability ($P(\text{disruption})$)**: A calibrated logistic model predicting binary 30-day disruption likelihood.
- **Governed Risk Score (0–100)**: A multi-dimensional policy-weighted index combining quality trends, capacity strain, single-source dependency, and external signal intensity.
- **Deterministic Impact Service**: Hard mathematical calculations of rupee financial exposure using unit contribution margins per Table 32. **Numerical calculations are never delegated to LLM generation**.

### 3. RAG Grounding & Honest Abstention
- Recommendations produced by the RAG Copilot must include clause-level citations to ingested contracts and corporate policies (`DOC-MSA-APEX-2025`, `DOC-POL-DUAL-SOURCE`).
- If evidence is insufficient, contradictory, or if a user submits an out-of-scope query, the platform **honestly abstains** (`abstained: true`) rather than fabricating ungrounded claims.

### 4. RBAC Policy Engine & Spending Authority Thresholds
- Human authorization is enforced on the server, not merely in the browser UI.
- Spend approvals strictly respect role-based statutory limits:
  - **Supply Chain Planner:** Spend up to ₹10,00,000 (₹10 Lakhs).
  - **Procurement Manager / Director:** Spend up to ₹50,00,000 (₹50 Lakhs).
  - **Chief Procurement Officer (CPO):** High-value mitigations exceeding ₹50 Lakhs.
